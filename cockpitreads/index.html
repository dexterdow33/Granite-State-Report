Standards

The pre-flight checklist for every story

Dexter Dow also runs Granite State Report, an independent New Hampshire news outlet. The same rules for sourcing apply here: if a fact can't be checked, it doesn't go in the script.

Visit Granite State Report
  • BEFORE EVERY READSTATUS
  • Own words, outlet named

    Stories are retold and explained, never read out verbatim. The original outlet gets credit on screen or aloud.

    CHECK
  • Quotes short and exact

    Direct quotes stay brief, word for word, and attributed to the person who said them.

    CHECK
  • Sources in the description

    Every video lists the links it used, and every link is opened and confirmed before upload.

    CHECK
  • Unknowns called out

    If something can't be confirmed yet, the video says so instead of guessing.

    CHECK
  • Own footage, cleared audio

    Visuals are original sim capture. Music comes from licensed or royalty-free libraries.

    CHECK
