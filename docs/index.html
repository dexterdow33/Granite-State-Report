About

Dexter Dow lives in New Hampshire and writes, publishes, and refuses to look away. The books take apart the systems most people live inside without seeing: surveillance economies, institutional decay, political capture.

The catalog spans artificial intelligence, political economy, media criticism, and geopolitics, treated as connected parts of one machine rather than separate subjects. Every title is independently published.

Dexter is the founder and editor of Granite State Report, an independent civic journalism outlet in New Hampshire.
